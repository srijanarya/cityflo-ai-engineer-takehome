# How others define "late": background research

Made in a separate Claude session on 2026-10-09, which searched the web and opened each source below. Srijan has not re-checked these sources.

| Source | What it says | Link |
|---|---|---|
| TRB survey of 146 US operators (1986) | Definitions vary widely. A window of no more than 1 minute early and 5 minutes late would cover most of them. | https://trid.trb.org/view.aspx?id=276035 |
| Banerjee et al. (2021) | There is still no US national standard. 1 minute early to 5 late is the most common window. SFMTA uses 1 early to 4 late; WMATA uses 2 early to 7 late. | https://arxiv.org/pdf/2112.11912 |
| TCQSM 2003, as reproduced in a Sound Transit EIS appendix | On time means 0 to 5 minutes late, and early does not count as on time. Grades run from A (95% or more on time) to F (under 75%). | https://www.soundtransit.org/sites/default/files/documents/pdf/projects/eastlink/eis_2011/18_apph1_appb_levelofservicedefinitionsusedforeastlinkanalysis.pdf |
| TfL (London) | Frequent routes are judged on Excess Wait Time. Less frequent routes are judged on the share of buses on time, defined as 2 minutes early to 5 late. | https://www.tfl.gov.uk/forms/14144.aspx |
| Ireland, tendered-bus contract schedules | On time is 1 minute early to 5:59 late. Missing AVL data is deducted unless the operator codes it within 5 days. Excluding exceptional events requires evidence. | https://www.nationaltransport.ie/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/ODMA-Schedules.pdf |
| India, CESL national e-bus contract (Sept 2022), Art. 20.4 and 29 | A trip arrives on time if it is within 10% of scheduled trip time, capped at 15 minutes; the target is 80% or more. Floods count as force majeure only with notice within 7 days and evidence. | https://tnidb.tn.gov.in/media/filer_public/cb/97/cb978e8c-bb0e-4fc4-a309-78a58e979991/c_cesl_sept_2022.pdf |
| FHWA travel-time reliability | Averages hide bad days. Commuters who plan on the average "will be late half the time". | https://ops.fhwa.dot.gov/publications/tt_reliability/TTR_Report.htm |
| Saavedra et al. (Waterloo) | Implausible AVL trips are flagged as suspect with a reason, not deleted. | https://uwspace.uwaterloo.ca/handle/10012/5417 |
| Google, GTFS-Realtime | Real-time data has a separate "no real-time data" state; stale feeds are discarded. | https://support.google.com/transitpartners/answer/10104663 |
| Cityflo subscription policy (updated 22 June 2026) | Free cancellation up to 30 minutes before pickup. There is no published lateness definition or late-bus refund. | https://www.cityflo.com/subscription-policy/ |
